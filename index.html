About the lab

A research lab with community at its core.

Led by Dr. Rebecca Mattson at San Diego State University, the Glass Lift Lab brings screening, data science, and community partnership together.

Our aim is to improve health for patients and families across San Diego through work shaped by both evidence and lived experience.
